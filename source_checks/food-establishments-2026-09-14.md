# Food-establishment approval lists

Three final configurations were each read twice through the engine: dairy (124 normalized approval numbers / 149 CSV rows), eggs (44 / 49), and meat of domestic ungulates (159 / 204). All initialized quietly, repeated without alerts and retained identical full state. The adjacent JSON records hashes of actual HTML/CSV reads and final module/configurations. These three sections constitute one configurable function.

The [official list index](https://www.mattilsynet.no/godkjente-produkter-og-virksomheter) links each selected section. The adapter discovers its current CSV attachment from that section page. It requires an exact official host, expected filename, section identity, strict CSV schema and the same multiset of approval IDs in the rendered HTML table and CSV. Size/row limits and malformed or incomplete responses fail before state changes. This validates the published selection, not completeness of every food establishment in the country.

Repeated approval numbers are genuine source structure. Distinct rows are retained together as canonically sorted entries; identical entries are coalesced. Leading/trailing whitespace is stripped from IDs, while internal spaces are preserved. Meat has 162 raw spellings, normalized to 159 numbers. Seven byte-equivalent dairy row duplicates are present. Row order and comma-separated code order do not produce alerts. Dates are validated; blank dates remain unknown. Multiple periods under one number remain separate entries.

Changes cover listed business names/towns, activity/production codes, associated activities, remarks, and available validity dates. Meat also includes species codes but does not publish the same validity fields. Street addresses, administrative county/municipality codes, ownership and inspection outcomes are outside this contract. Raw code labels are preserved without invented interpretation. First observation does not establish a new approval date. Disappearance does not prove withdrawal and does not trigger a removal alert. The same number in different sections remains section-specific; cross-section deduplication is not claimed.

Artificial tests cover grouped entries, quiet repeats, revisions/additions, date/schema/identity failures, redirects and size limits. No later actual approval change or scheduled-repeat delivery is established by these public-read checks.
