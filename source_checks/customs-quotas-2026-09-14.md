# Customs quota capacity and calculated remaining bands

Two real reads through the final recipe returned 10 stable quota identities. The local engine produced zero alerts during initialization and the identical repeat. The companion JSON records configuration/module hashes and public response hashes.

The [official dataset](https://data.toll.no/no/dataset/tollkvote) publishes version 1.0 with the complete returned `tollkvoter` array. Identity is `dokumentnummer`; selected fields are maximum quantity, country-group code, commodity codes, unit and validity dates. Empty, duplicated, oversized or malformed input fails before state advances. Unsupported versions or envelope fields are rejected rather than silently treating future pagination as complete. No removals or full historical coverage are claimed.

Calculated remaining quantity is `maksMengde - avskrevetMengde`. At the recipe's 10-percent boundary, positive remaining quantities at or below 10 percent of capacity are classified as low; zero/negative is exhausted or overdrawn. These are calculated bands, not an official guarantee of quota availability for a particular import. The public metadata does not further define accounting semantics of the deducted quantity. Corrections can move a quota back to a higher band. Alerts identify the calculation. Raw deducted and calculated remaining values are retained in observations and displayed, but small movements inside one band do not themselves change the notification fingerprint. Capacity, unit, commodity, country-group or validity changes remain independently monitored.

The two observed unit labels are `K` / `Kg` and `V` / `Verdi`. No currency is specified for Verdi, so alerts explicitly avoid assigning one. Group and commodity codes are preserved without an inferred geographic or product translation. Validity boundaries are shown as source data; publication date is left unset. There is no importer identity, allocation decision or auction price in this dataset. Links open the official dataset page.

Tests cover quiet initialization/repeat, low/exhausted/reopened band changes, capacity/validity changes, new quota identity, explicit unit handling, decimal/order/metadata noise, negative derived remaining quantity, and fail-closed schema/amount/date/identity/size guards. Synthetic changes prove behavior; only the two public reads prove current source compatibility. Later hosted recurrence and real notification delivery remain separate evidence requirements.
