# Consultation metadata and published responses

Two real polls of the final recipes read two explicitly selected consultation cases and three institutional responses in one case. Both polls produced zero alerts and identical full state. Exact URLs, response sizes/hashes, configuration and final adapter hash are in [the evidence JSON](consultations-2026-09-14.json). Ordinary TLS was used, without a notifier. The cases currently publish deadlines of 6 October and 20 November 2026 and status “På høring”. These are source observations, not legal conclusions or proof of future changes.

The metadata profile monitors case title, stated status and calendar deadline. It requires an unambiguous status block and canonical case identity; a date does not imply a specific closing time. The response profile requires an unfiltered list whose row count matches its reported total, then reads selected response details and verifies that the whole list remains unchanged. The selected case lists six responses; three labelled Privatperson are excluded by default after validating all six entries. The three selected institutional responses are currently without comments. Response UUIDs preserve identity across text, type, date and attachment changes. Normalized body text is hashed rather than copied into alerts or state. Attachment identities and titles are monitored, but their file contents are not fetched.

A separate real response from another case verified nonempty comment text and one attachment link. That case’s list contains 40 rows including direct PDF submissions with a name in place of a response UUID. Such mixed lists are deliberately rejected by this adapter. Matching a displayed total does not prove support for pagination, other list types or all cases; partial, paginated, filtered, duplicate and mixed-format lists fail before state advances. No unsupported entries are silently skipped.

Seven adapter tests cover quiet repeats, changed deadline/status, new responses, changed body/attachment/type, default person exclusion, exact identity, invalid dates, unsupported types, out-of-scope attachments, duplicate/partial/filtered and changing lists, byte/record limits and redirects. Together with recipe and catalog tests, 25 targeted checks passed. Artificial changes establish event behavior only. These reads do not establish a later actual response or deadline change delivered in operation. Absence never asserts deletion, and later decisions are outside coverage. Both profiles form one consultation-follow-up function rather than two independently counted functions.
