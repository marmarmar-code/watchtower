# PRAC safety-signal recommendation actions

The [official EMA index](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/human-regulatory-overview/post-authorisation/pharmacovigilance-post-authorisation/signal-management/prac-recommendations-safety-signals) publishes meeting recommendation documents. The adapter selects the two newest meetings from 2026 onward, validates the index metadata, and reads their English PDFs. This is recommendation-action monitoring, separate from product authorisation metadata, shortages and recalls.

Two real final polls each read the index twice and both PDFs twice. The July meeting document contained ten entries (two product-information recommendations, six supplementary-information entries and two other recommendations); June contained thirteen (five, seven and one). Together, 23 rows were quietly initialized and a second local poll produced no alerts and identical full state. The adjacent JSON records all twelve response hashes and the final code/configuration fingerprints. Fifty-five targeted tests passed, including actual decoding of generic in-memory PDFs and separately mocked transport/state cases. Real documents were also checked visually. Synthetic fixtures are not real-data proof.

Identity combines the document reference, category and EPITT identifier. One July EPITT appears in two categories and remains two separate records. Meeting dates, individual adoption dates and the index's literal first-publication dates remain distinct. No publication time is inferred. The action and MAH cells retain their text, including multiple lines/actions within a merged record; their contents are not expanded into inferred legal obligations or calculated deadlines.

The PDF contract requires bounded digital A4 pages, matching document references and page numbers, the three explicit sections, consecutive product-information item numbers, exact metadata labels and five named table columns. The parser uses enclosing coloured cell geometry rather than rectangles repeated behind each text line. Counts of every visible EPITT occurrence must match extracted rows. Missing cells, unfamiliar layout, duplicate identities, document drift between reads, or regression of the newest meeting fail before history advances. The parser version is pinned because text and geometry extraction are part of this source contract.

Product-information recommendations retain their introductory action text up to the proposed product-characteristics wording. The proposed wording itself contains meaningful underlining and strikethrough, so it is excluded from plain-text monitoring and must be read in the original PDF. Pure formatting changes are not monitored as semantic changes. Signal labels do not independently establish clinical causation or harm, and a PRAC recommendation is not proof of an implemented product change.

First observation is quiet. Added and changed recommendation actions may alert once. Falling out of the two-document window is not withdrawal. The source layout is deliberately strict and may require maintenance after an EMA format change. Later scheduled repetition and actual delivery are not established by these local checks.
