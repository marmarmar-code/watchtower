# Public REMIT enforcement report

The official [ACER enforcement page](https://www.acer.europa.eu/remit/coordination-on-cases/enforcement-decisions) and its anonymous embedded report were read on 18 September 2026. Two complete adapter polls each repeated the page, embed, model, table and independent count query: 20 successful HTTP reads in total.

Each table contained 148 projected rows, matching 148 independently counted non-null participant cells. The default selection from 2024 contained 39 report groups and 39 underposts. Silent initial evaluation and repeated evaluation produced zero alerts and identical full state. The reported model refresh was 15 September 2026 at 08:47:57.973 UTC. Cache and request timestamps do not enter alert fingerprints.

All 148 historical rows produce 145 composite groups; three groups contain two distinct underposts. The adapter retains both monetary/status rows in each group, and does not invent case identifiers or aggregate amounts. Composite identity is year, authority, participant, breach reference and national source link. A correction to one of those identity fields can appear as a newly observed report group; there is no official case ID in the observed table.

This adds one distinct function: structured REMIT enforcement status and reaction monitoring. It is not an additional company selection, RSS feed or electricity outage function. Amount strings can describe warnings, confiscation, reimbursement, currency conversions or proposed sanctions. They remain source text. Year is not a full decision date, and the independent count measures published report rows with populated participant fields, not all national enforcement decisions.

Unknown schema/envelope formats, truncation, absent footnotes, count mismatch, stale/regressed model refresh, conflicting double reads and a full row window stop ingestion. Initial reads are silent, and absent rows do not imply withdrawal or acquittal. This source check proves retrieval and local state behavior. It does not prove deployment, a genuinely new event, scheduled repetition, delivery or editorial acceptance. No test notification was sent.
