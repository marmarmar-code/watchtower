# Public financial declarations

The [official EU register guidelines](https://transparency-register.europa.eu/guidelines_en) distinguish estimated activity costs from the total budgets disclosed by noncommercial organisations. These are self-reported declarations, with explicit euro currency. The adapter monitors financial declarations rather than general contact or update fields.

The final recipe read the full official XML export twice per poll. Two actual polls downloaded four identical 115,817,076-byte responses containing 17,777 unique register identities and selected 156 entries with head-office country NORWAY. Both polls produced zero alerts and identical next state. There were 119 closed-year cost-range declarations, 32 total-budget declarations and five new organisations without a closed year. Adjacent JSON records hashes and final code/configuration fingerprints. Fifty-one targeted synthetic contract and integration tests passed separately from this real-data check.

The source uses XML 1.1. Its root is namespaced, while metaData and resultList explicitly reset the default namespace. Eight numeric control references valid under [XML 1.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/xml11/) occur in unmonitored descriptive fields. A bounded compatibility view replaces those references with explicit markers for Expat; every monitored value rejects such a marker. Raw downloads remain unchanged and outside this repository. Entity declarations, unexpected structures, duplicate identities, inconsistent counts, stale or regressing exports, and mismatched complete reads preserve prior state.

Amounts retain their declared type and euro currency. Missing range bounds remain absent rather than becoming zero. Source financial-period dates remain literal; no calendar year is inferred for the undated current-year section. Identical grants retain multiplicity. Estimated activity costs, intermediary ranges and total organisation budgets are not added together. Unsupported financial models fail explicitly.

Record links use the official organisation-detail page and the register identity. Initial observation is quiet. New entries and changes may alert once; disappearance is not proof that activity ceased. The bounded full-export download is larger than ordinary page sources. Two matching reads do not establish a transactional snapshot. Later scheduled operation, new delivery, financial accuracy and influence outcomes are not established by this local check.
